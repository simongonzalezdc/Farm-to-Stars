# Future Expansions - Farm to Stars

## Overview
This document tracks features, content, and systems planned for future releases after the initial launch.

---

## Civilization Expansion Pack

### Status: Planned for Post-Launch
**Target:** 6-12 months after initial release

### Additional Civilizations (5 total)

#### 1. Zapotec Confederation
**Region:** Oaxaca highlands (Mesoamerica)
**Playstyle:** Weather/Atmospheric control
**Core Mechanic:** Altitude-based farming, weather prediction
**Visual Theme:** Sky blue/white, cloud patterns, terraced architecture

**Bonuses:**
- +15% crop yield at high altitudes
- Weather events predicted 2 days in advance
- +10% atmospheric research speed

**Unique Building:** Sky Observatory (predicts weather, boosts research)

**Lore Hook:** "Cloud People who mastered the sky and became planetary engineers"

---

#### 2. Nazca Confederation
**Region:** Southern Peru desert (South America)
**Playstyle:** Surveying/Precision/Planning
**Core Mechanic:** Enhanced terrain visibility, perfect planning tools
**Visual Theme:** Geometric patterns, lines, earth tones with purple accents

**Bonuses:**
- +25% surveying range/accuracy
- Can see underground resources
- +10% construction precision (reduced waste)

**Unique Building:** Geoglyph Array (reveals map area, boosts navigation)

**Lore Hook:** "Line Makers who mapped the cosmos and surveyed the solar system"

---

#### 3. Tiwanaku Empire
**Region:** Bolivian Altiplano (South America)
**Playstyle:** Construction/Megastructures/Durability
**Core Mechanic:** Buildings have increased durability, can build larger structures
**Visual Theme:** Massive stone blocks, gray/brown, monumental scale

**Bonuses:**
- +20% building durability
- +15% construction of large buildings
- Buildings require less repair

**Unique Building:** Monumental Gateway (increases city prestige, durability aura)

**Lore Hook:** "Stone Masters who built lunar bases and Martian monuments"

---

#### 4. Wari Empire
**Region:** Andean highlands (South America)
**Playstyle:** Communication/Networking/Coordination
**Core Mechanic:** Enhanced trade routes, instant communication, network effects
**Visual Theme:** Network patterns, roads, red/gold, interconnected design

**Bonuses:**
- +30% trade route efficiency
- Instant communication between settlements
- +15% to all bonuses when connected to network

**Unique Building:** Road Network Hub (connects distant settlements, multiplies effects)

**Lore Hook:** "Network Builders who created the solar system internet"

---

#### 5. Haudenosaunee Confederacy
**Region:** Northeastern woodlands (North America)
**Playstyle:** Diplomacy/Democratic governance/Unity
**Core Mechanic:** Democratic decision-making, diplomatic relations, alliance bonuses
**Visual Theme:** Longhouse architecture, natural wood, democratic symbols

**Bonuses:**
- +20% diplomatic relations with all factions
- Democratic decisions provide stability bonuses
- +15% cooperation/alliance benefits

**Unique Building:** Great Longhouse (council chamber, boosts happiness and diplomacy)

**Lore Hook:** "People of the Longhouse whose democracy guided humanity to the stars"

---

## Implementation Priority

### Phase 1: High-Altitude Specialists
- **Zapotec** (weather/altitude)
- **Tiwanaku** (construction/durability)

**Rationale:** Adds vertical gameplay dimension, complements existing civs

### Phase 2: Network & Exploration
- **Wari** (communication/networks)
- **Nazca** (surveying/planning)

**Rationale:** Adds late-game optimization strategies

### Phase 3: Diplomacy & Endgame
- **Haudenosaunee** (diplomacy/governance)

**Rationale:** Sets up Nation phase diplomatic systems, prepares for 1492 Contact Event

### Space Phase Content (Year 3+)
- Solar system colonization
- Civilization specializations in space:
  - Teotihuacan: Solar arrays on Mercury/Venus
  - Maya: Deep space observatories, Timeship research
  - Zapotec: Venus cloud cities, terraforming
  - Moche: Europa ocean colonies, water extraction
  - Nazca: Asteroid belt surveying, Kuiper Belt observation
  - Tiwanaku: Lunar/Martian megastructure construction
  - Wari: Solar system communication network
  - Hopewell: Post-scarcity resource sharing
  - Puebloan: Closed-loop space habitats
  - Haudenosaunee: Galactic diplomatic corps
- The Signal event (cosmic first contact)
- Multiple cosmic endings

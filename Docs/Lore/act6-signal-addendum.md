### Lesson 5: Balance Caution and Openness

**1492 Scenario:**
- Too cautious = miss opportunities for knowledge exchange
- Too open = appear naive/exploitable

- Too welcoming = potentially invite hostile action
- Balanced approach = monitor closely, respond carefully
- Let them demonstrate intentions through actions

**Game mechanic:**
Player's 1492 approach (militarist, diplomatic, isolationist, open) influences available strategies in Act 6. Past lessons shape current options.
